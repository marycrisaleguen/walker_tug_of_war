## Why This Tier Matters

NumPy is the foundation of numerical computing in Python. Pandas makes tabular data manipulation intuitive. Matplotlib and Seaborn turn raw numbers into insights. Together they form the data scientist's daily toolbox.

---

## Learning Outcomes

After completing this tier you will be able to:

- Manipulate multi-dimensional arrays efficiently with NumPy
- Load, clean, aggregate, and transform datasets with Pandas
- Create publication-quality visualizations to understand data distributions and relationships
