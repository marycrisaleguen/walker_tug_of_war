---

## Why This Tier Matters

A model is only as good as its evaluation. Choosing the wrong metric or evaluation strategy can lead you to believe a bad model is good — and deploy it to production.

---

## Learning Outcomes

After completing this tier you will be able to:

- Design a proper train/validation/test split strategy for any dataset
- Choose the right evaluation metric for a business problem
- Diagnose underfitting and overfitting using learning curves
