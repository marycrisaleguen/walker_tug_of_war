## Why This Tier Matters

Language is sequential. RNNs and their gated variants (LSTM, GRU) were the dominant approach to NLP tasks before Transformers and still appear in many time-series and signal-processing applications.

---

## Learning Outcomes

After completing this tier you will be able to:

- Build sequence models for text classification and generation
- Preprocess raw text into model-ready feature representations
- Use pretrained word embeddings to leverage external knowledge
