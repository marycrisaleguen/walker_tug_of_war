## Why This Tier Matters

Transformers are the foundation of large language models (GPT, BERT, LLaMA). GANs and diffusion models generate realistic images and audio. Reinforcement learning enables autonomous agents.

---

## Learning Outcomes

After completing this tier you will be able to:

- Implement and fine-tune Transformer-based models for NLP tasks
- Train a GAN or VAE for image generation
- Apply Q-learning and policy gradient methods to simple RL environments
