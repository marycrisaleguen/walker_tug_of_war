## Why This Tier Matters

Most real-world data is unlabeled. Clustering finds natural groupings (customer segments, anomaly detection), and dimensionality reduction enables visualization and speeds up downstream models.

---

## Learning Outcomes

After completing this tier you will be able to:

- Cluster datasets and evaluate cluster quality
- Reduce high-dimensional data to 2D/3D for visualization
- Apply dimensionality reduction as a preprocessing step
